Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

General information

This website presents Fate Sports Group and its work. It does not use newsletters or marketing tools. When you send one of our enquiry forms, or leave your details on one of our team’s digital business cards, what you enter is delivered to us by email through our form provider, Formspree, and is used only to reply to you and follow up.

Technical data

When you browse the website, technical data such as your IP address, browser type and request logs may be processed automatically by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, for:

  • Security and protection against attacks
  • Error detection
  • Performance

This data is not used for commercial or marketing purposes.

Cookies and storage

This website does not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, and loads no third-party fonts or trackers. It keeps a small note in your browser for the current visit only (session storage) so the opening animation is not repeated; this is deleted when you close the tab. The digital business card pages also keep a note in your browser (local storage), described below. As a result, no cookie consent banner is required.

Digital business cards

Our team’s digital business cards (pages under fatesportsgroup.com/card) ask for your name and email, and optionally your company and phone number, before showing the card. These details are sent to Fate Sports Group by email through Formspree, so the person whose card you opened can follow up with you. Your phone also keeps them in its browser storage (local storage) so you are not asked again when you open a Fate card; you can remove them at any time by clearing website data for fatesportsgroup.com. We do not add these details to marketing lists, and we do not sell or share them.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to handle your enquiry. We keep them for as long as needed for that purpose.

Legal basis

Processing is based on our legitimate interest in running a secure website and answering enquiries, in accordance with the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and applicable Monaco data protection regulations.

Data sharing

No personal data is sold, rented or shared with third parties, except where required by law, for hosting and security purposes, or to deliver form messages to us (Formspree).

Your rights

You can ask to access your personal data, have it corrected or deleted, or object to or limit its processing. Send requests to us by email: Email us. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect legal or technical changes. The latest version is always on this page.
