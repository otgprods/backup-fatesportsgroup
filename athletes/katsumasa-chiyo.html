How we work with brands

At FATE, every athlete's journey is built individually, with clarity, intent, and purpose. We work one-to-one, founded on trust and connection, guiding each athlete through every stage of their career with conviction. This is where ambition meets strategy.

While motorsport is where our story begins, our expertise expands into the wider world of global sports.

Drivermanagement

We manage drivers across multiple global regions.

We represent established professional athletes, factory drivers and emerging talent, supporting careers across single-seaters, GT racing, and endurance competition. Our approach is performance-led and long-term, protecting our clients' interests today while building sustainable careers for the future.

We manage every stage of an athlete's career, combining sporting insight with commercial, contractual, and strategic expertise. From rising talent to proven professionals, our role is to provide clarity, protection, and opportunity through every phase of development and success.

    Former professional racing driver, turned sports agent, Struan transitioned into sports management after honing his expertise at one of the leading agencies in the motorsport industry.     Le Mans 24 Hour LMP2 winner and former professional GT driver, Chris is now a highly respected sports agent within motorsport, amongst other sports. With decades of experience at the highest level in racing, he specialises in driver management, partnerships, brand activation, and commercial strategy.       Driver management

      Our services& structure

      Every athlete is treated on an individual basis - there is no fixed model.

      Professional drivers typically engage through a tailored retainer and/or performance-based commission structure, aligned to income streams and long-term career value. Drivers funding their own programmes benefit from structured management support, ensuring professional-level guidance on where and how to invest, protecting budgets and maximising return.

      Aspiring drivers and early-stage talent may enter through a progressive fee model aligned to performance, potential and long-term development. This approach allows FATE Sports Group to support elite professionals and future stars with the same level of commitment, discretion and ambition.
